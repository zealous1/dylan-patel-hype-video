What the original is doing, measured

From analysis/flicker. The original stacks several old-film effects. None of them is a filter preset you'd name on its own.

LayerIn the originalIn our passes
Strobe23.50 to 23.70, every other frame swings between 125 and 110 mean luma, about 13%. That's a 15 Hz strobe on the jersey hit.Borrowed. We read their per-frame exposure over a running median and apply the same wobble to our frames, so the strobe lands on the same frames.
FlickerOn the end card, frame-to-frame exposure jitters 2 to 10% with no regular period (ratios 0.90 to 1.10). The clipped whites stay put, so the flicker lives in the face and the midtones.Borrowed in all three. Worn adds its own random projector flicker on top.
Black floorNothing reaches black. The 2nd percentile sits at 22 to 25 out of 255, a flat dark grey, while contrast is crushed hard.An S curve, then blacks lifted to 20 to 26.
ScratchesThin vertical lines, white and dark, appear for a few frames on the jersey and through the logo. The vertical white line through his head that we chased earlier is one of these, and it's in the original.Scratches that live 3 to 14 frames and drift sideways.
DustSpecks and dots, most visible on the swoosh, one frame each.One-frame specks and the odd hair, 60% dark.
ColourSkin and swoosh go warm yellow-orange, saturation breathes from 15 to 52 as the logo comes in.Ours keeps our grade. Classic and Worn add red halation around the highlights.

The order matters, because a real print gains each one at a different stage. Lens and camera come first, then the negative, the print and the projector.
