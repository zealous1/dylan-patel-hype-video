Upscale sweep

Every scene whose picture comes from a 1280x720 Wan generation, upscaled with SeedVR2 and judged against the plain Lanczos resize the reel uses now. Four 1080 variants and one 1440 variant ran on Modal, each over the whole generation in one batch. C (7B sharp at 1440, Lanczos down to 1080) wins every scene. The 1080 runs (A, B, D) all leave a dark line in the bottom row and a bright line above it, because SeedVR2 pads 1080 to 1088 and crops the seam back in. 1440 needs no padding and its edges are clean. Beyond that C has the most detail on fadeout, alleyoop, backdown and fadeaway, and its shimmer per unit of detail is at or below the Lanczos baseline on every shot.

The rebuilt takes reuse each approved take's per-frame blur picks (lookmatch --blur-from), so the whips, zoom blurs and flash frames stay exactly as they were. A plain lookmatch rerun re-derives those picks from the sharper source, which dropped the radial whip on rim's last frame and blurred rim straight back to its old sharpness.

Shimmer is the mean frame-to-frame change of the high-pass on the 20% stillest 64 px tiles; shimmer per detail divides it by that tile's detail, so a sharper result with steady texture keeps it level. Laplacian variance and detail skip the bottom 56 rows, where the 1080 runs have their seam.

GPU seconds run from function entry to the saved output at Modal list price. The billed rows are Modal's own figure per app, which adds container boot and image load, and include two batches that produced nothing (one lost its client, one failed to save its outputs) and a smoke test.
