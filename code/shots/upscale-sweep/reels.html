Upscale options, full reels

Four renders of the Worn 30 edit, 902 frames each. They differ only in how the shots that came from 720p AI video were brought up to 1080p. Every other scene, the newspaper (paper-v5, built from the C celebrate) and the mix are the same in all four.

Lanczos
The plain resize, the no-AI baseline. It scales the 1280x720 pixels up with a standard filter and invents no detail, so it is faithful but soft. These are the takes the reel used before the upscale work.
A, B, C
SeedVR2, an AI upscaler that redraws the frame at the higher size. A is its 7B model at 1080. B is the 7B "sharp" model at 1080. C is the 7B sharp model at 1440, then scaled down to 1080. C is what the v2 reel uses.
Sharpness
The number in the tables is Laplacian variance, a measure of fine edge energy. Higher means more fine detail in the frame. It also rises with noise and grain, so a higher number is not automatically better; it has to be read next to shimmer and checked by eye.
Shimmer
How much the fine texture changes from one frame to the next in parts of the picture that are not moving. An upscaler that redraws detail slightly differently each frame makes still areas crawl or boil. "Shimmer per detail" divides that by how much detail there is, so a sharper result with steady texture scores the same as the baseline, and one that boils scores higher.
The green row
In the per-shot tables on the sweep page, the green row is my pick for that shot, weighing detail, shimmer, faithfulness and defects together. It is not simply the highest number. For example the 1080 runs (A, B) leave a dark line along the bottom edge of every frame, which no score shows, and that is a large part of why C was picked.

Backdown and fadeaway were only upscaled with C, so the A and B reels use their Lanczos takes for those two shots. In the A and B reels only rim, celebrate, alleyoop and fadeout change.
