60 fps routes for the LeBron reel

Every route is the full reel at 60 fps before the film pass, 1804 frames. The players stay on the same frame when you switch routes. Keys: ← → step one frame, with Shift ten, Space plays or pauses, F swaps the two panes in place, 1 shows one pane or two.

Measures

Measured on every in-between frame (the odd 60 fps frames). Sharpness is the variance of the Laplacian. Ratio is an in-between's sharpness over the geometric mean of its two neighbours, so 1.00 means as sharp as the real frames; Text is the newspaper and the podcast cut, Rest everything else. Soft counts in-betweens under 0.70. Judder is the mean size of that ratio's log2, so it counts frames that are softer and frames that are crisper than their neighbours alike, which is what reads as flicker. Cadence is how unevenly a frame splits the motion between its neighbours, 0 for an even split and 1 for a held frame, over in-betweens with motion.
